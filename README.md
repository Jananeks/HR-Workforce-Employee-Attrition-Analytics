# HR Workforce & Employee Attrition Analytics

## 📊 Project Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze employee workforce composition, attrition, compensation, performance, overtime, and employee retention risk.

The dashboard contains four analytical pages and uses DAX, data modeling, interactive visualizations, and synchronized slicers to provide actionable HR insights.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to help HR teams:

- Monitor overall workforce size and employee exits
- Analyze employee attrition patterns
- Identify major reasons for employee exits
- Compare attrition across departments and job roles
- Analyze salary and tenure-related attrition
- Evaluate employee performance and overtime
- Analyze promotion and workforce characteristics
- Identify employees requiring retention attention
- Support data-driven workforce planning

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

- The dataset contains **1,000 employees**, with **760 active employees** and **240 exited employees**.
- Employees with **1–3 years of tenure** showed the highest attrition rate at approximately **26%**.
- **Compensation** and **work-life balance** were among the leading employee exit reasons.
- Attrition varied across departments, age groups, salary bands, tenure groups, and locations.
- Overtime levels differed across departments, helping identify areas where workload may require further investigation.
- Employee performance and training were analyzed to identify workforce development patterns.

---

## ⚠️ Employee Risk Classification

A rule-based employee risk classification was developed to identify employees who may require additional HR attention.

The risk score considers:

- Overtime hours
- Employee tenure
- Promotion status
- Performance rating

Employees were classified into:

- **High Risk**
- **Medium Risk**
- **Low Risk**

The resulting classification was:

- High Risk: **350 employees**
- Medium Risk: **635 employees**
- Low Risk: **15 employees**

> **Note:** This is a rule-based analytical classification and is not a predictive machine-learning model.
